## 1. Request

The borrower requests a Senior unsecured term loan of $500 million with a 5-year tenor, for General corporate purposes. This is a hypothetical request at $500 million, used to test the borrower's capacity. We assume pricing at 5.10%, the coupon on the company's most recent senior unsecured issue [10-K Debt note ¶32, ¶33, p. 70](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). The Norfolk Southern acquisition is expected to close in 2027 [10-K Acquisition note ¶8, p. 75](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm), so most of the 5-year tenor would run against the combined company.

## 2. Borrower overview

Union Pacific Railroad is the main operating company of the group and a Class I railroad serving the western two-thirds of the United States [10-K Item 1 ¶3, ¶16, p. 6](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Freight is spread across three groups, Bulk, Industrial and Premium, with no single dominant category [10-K Item 1 ¶18, ¶19, ¶21, p. 6](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). The company reports the Railroad as one segment because the network is integrated [10-K Item 1 ¶14, p. 6](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). FY2025 revenue was $24.5 billion. BNSF is its main rail competitor and runs parallel routes in many of its main corridors [10-K Item 1A ¶20, p. 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Most employees belong to unions and work under collective bargaining agreements [10-K Item 1A ¶13, p. 12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). In 2025 the company posted its best-ever personal injury and derailment rates, and service metrics also improved [10-K Item 7 ¶11, ¶12, p. 29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). In July 2025 it signed a merger agreement to acquire Norfolk Southern [10-K Acquisition note ¶3, p. 74](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). The combined railroad would be America's first transcontinental railroad [10-K Acquisition note ¶5, p. 75](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).

## 3. Financial analysis

**Earnings.** Revenue grew 0.5% in FY2024 and 1.1% in FY2025. EBITDA grew faster, by 6.2% and then 1.7%, reaching $12.3 billion. The EBITDA margin widened from 47.3% to 50.2%. Management credits core pricing, productivity and volume growth, partly offset by inflation, mix and acquisition costs [10-K Item 7 ¶14, p. 29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Our EBITDA excludes other income of $629 million, which was lifted by industrial park land sales [10-K Item 7 ¶120, p. 34](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).

**Leverage.** Debt/EBITDA improved from 2.86x to 2.58x. Total debt fell 4.3% in FY2024, then rose 2.0% in FY2025 to $31.8 billion. Lease-adjusted leverage tracked this at 2.60x. Equity grew to $18.5 billion.

**Coverage.** Interest expense held at $1.3 billion, giving coverage of 9.40x. The company had no variable-rate debt at year end [10-K Item 7A ¶4, p. 39](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).

**Cash flow.** CFO fell 0.6% to $9.3 billion, because of the timing of tax payments and purchased tax credits [10-K Item 7, Liquidity ¶29, p. 37](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Cash taxes fell to $877 million from $1.5 billion, helped by the permanent return of full bonus depreciation [10-K Item 7, Liquidity ¶30, p. 38](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). After capex of $3.8 billion, free cash flow was $5.5 billion. Dividends of $3.2 billion and buybacks of $2.7 billion together exceeded that free cash flow. Debt issuance of $2.0 billion covered the gap, and debt repaid was $1.4 billion. Buybacks were paused after the merger was announced, while dividends continue [10-K Item 7 ¶193, p. 36](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).

**Liquidity.** The current ratio is 0.91x, a working capital deficit that management attributes to upcoming debt maturities [10-K Item 7, Liquidity ¶3, p. 36](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Liquidity consists of cash of $1.3 billion, an undrawn revolver of $2.0 billion, and $600 million undrawn on the Receivables Facility [10-K Item 7, Liquidity ¶5, p. 37](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Scheduled principal is $1.5 billion. Under the revolver covenant, debt as defined was $33.5 billion against $47.7 billion allowed [10-K Debt note ¶27, p. 70](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).

**Pro forma.** Adding $500 million at 5.10% costs $26 million of interest a year and lifts total debt to $32.3 billion. Pro forma debt/EBITDA becomes 2.62x and lease-adjusted leverage 2.64x. Interest coverage would be 9.23x, DSCR 2.68x and the current ratio 0.91x. These pro forma figures cover Union Pacific standalone, because the Norfolk Southern deal has not yet closed [10-K Acquisition note ¶8, p. 75](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).

## 4. Strengths

- **Franchise and margins:** the EBITDA margin of 50.2% reflects core pricing gains and strong productivity [10-K Item 7 ¶14, p. 29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm), on a network spanning the western two-thirds of the country [10-K Item 1 ¶3, p. 6](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).
- **Debt capacity:** debt as defined is $33.5 billion against a covenant ceiling of $47.7 billion [10-K Debt note ¶27, p. 70](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm), and the revolver has no rating triggers apart from pricing [10-K Debt note ¶27, p. 70](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).
- **Rate risk:** 0.0% of debt is floating [10-K Item 7A ¶4, p. 39](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).
- **Maturity profile:** annual maturities through 2030 are small compared with CFO of $9.3 billion [10-K Debt note ¶17, ¶18, ¶19, ¶20, ¶21, p. 69](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).
- **Ratings:** S&P A- (no dated action found), Moody's A3 stable (last action 2025-07-29) and Fitch A- (last action 2026-07-15).
- **Fuel pass-through:** fuel surcharges recover a significant share of fuel costs [10-K Item 1A ¶26, p. 15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).

## 5. Risks and mitigants

- **Norfolk Southern acquisition (main risk).** About $20.0 billion of cash consideration is to be funded with new debt and operating cash [10-K Acquisition note ¶4, p. 74](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Closing needs STB approval and is expected in 2027 [10-K Acquisition note ¶7, ¶8, p. 75](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). The combined company will carry more debt, and servicing it will take more cash than either company needs today [10-K Item 1A ¶65, p. 19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). The merger financing is expected to include a leverage covenant [10-K Item 1A ¶66, ¶67, p. 19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). *Mitigants:* buybacks are paused [10-K Item 7 ¶193, p. 36](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm), and covenant headroom of $47.7 billion against $33.5 billion is large [10-K Debt note ¶27, p. 70](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Beyond the $20.0 billion funding need, we cannot size combined leverage, because no Norfolk Southern figures are available.
- **Deal failure.** If the deal fails, the company could owe a termination fee of $2.5 billion [10-K Acquisition note ¶17, p. 75](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). *Mitigant:* the fee equals less than one year of free cash flow of $5.5 billion.
- **Integration and regulation.** Neither company has completed a deal of this size [10-K Item 1A ¶50, p. 18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). The STB may impose conditions that reduce the benefits [10-K Item 1A ¶41, ¶42, p. 16, 17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). *Mitigant:* the standalone business is performing well on safety and service [10-K Item 7 ¶11, ¶12, p. 29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).
- **Refinancing.** The revolver expires in 2027 [10-K Debt note ¶26, p. 69](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm), around the expected closing [10-K Acquisition note ¶8, p. 75](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). If bond ratings fell below investment grade, access to the Receivables Facility could be reduced [10-K Item 7, Liquidity ¶5, p. 37](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).
- **Operations and macro.** The main exposures are hazardous materials [10-K Item 1A ¶6, p. 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm), labor disputes [10-K Item 1A ¶13, p. 12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm), weather [10-K Item 1A ¶9, p. 11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm) and trade disruption [10-K Item 1A ¶10, p. 11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Management expects macro uncertainty in 2026, with industrial production forecast flat [10-K Item 7 ¶26, p. 30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). *Mitigant:* the 2026 capital plan is lower than FY2025 capex of $3.8 billion [10-K Item 7, Liquidity ¶58, p. 38](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).

## 6. Stress test

The scenario applies a revenue decline of 20.0%, a margin cut of 300bp (from 50.2% to 47.2%), and a rate shock of 200bp. Stressed EBITDA is $9.3 billion. Debt/EBITDA rises to 3.44x and lease-adjusted leverage to 3.43x. Interest coverage falls to 7.07x, DSCR to 1.62x, and the current ratio stays at 0.91x. No grading threshold breaks, so stress adds 0 notches. Extra interest from the rate shock is $0, because 0.0% of existing debt is floating [10-K Item 7A ¶4, p. 39](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). We treat that $0 as a lower bound, since the term loan or the merger debt may carry floating rates. With the facility added, stressed leverage is 3.49x, coverage 6.94x and DSCR 1.61x. The facility's stressed extra interest is shown as $0, which also understates cost if the loan floats.

## 8. Recommendation

I recommend approval. The $500 million exposure is small against covenant headroom of $47.7 billion versus $33.5 billion outstanding [10-K Debt note ¶27, p. 70](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Even stressed and pro forma, DSCR holds at 1.61x and coverage at 6.94x. A grade of 5 understates standalone quality, because the current ratio score of 8 reflects a structural working capital deficit rather than weak liquidity [10-K Item 7, Liquidity ¶3, p. 36](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). The real uncertainty is the post-merger balance sheet, which we cannot measure today [10-K Acquisition note ¶4, p. 74](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm). Structure should therefore protect us if combined leverage overshoots *(analyst judgment)*. Approval should not be repeated without combined-company projections *(analyst judgment)*.

Recommendation: Approve with conditions:
- (a) the loan ranks pari passu with senior unsecured debt priced at least at 5.10%;
- (b) the loan carries an adjusted debt-to-EBITDA covenant and cross-default no looser than the revolver's [10-K Debt note ¶26, ¶27, p. 69, 70](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm);
- (c) a ratings-based pricing grid applies, plus a prepayment right if ratings fall below investment grade [10-K Item 7, Liquidity ¶5, p. 37](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm);
- (d) the borrower delivers the merger financing plan and combined projections, and we re-grade at closing [10-K Acquisition note ¶4, ¶8, p. 74, 75](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm);
- (e) any floating-rate exposure on this loan is hedged, given the 200bp stress [10-K Item 7A ¶4, p. 39](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/100885/000010088526000037/unp-20251231.htm).
